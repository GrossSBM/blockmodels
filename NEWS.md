# blockmodels 1.2.1

* Added covariates on the nodes, driving the block memberships through a multinomial logistic regression (argument `nodes_covariates` of all the models: a list with one matrix named `node` for SBM and SBM_sym, or with matrices named `row` and/or `col` for LBM). The estimated coefficients are stored in the field `B` (and `G` for the columns of an LBM) of the memberships, and the ICL penalty accounts for them.
* Beginning adding handling of `NA` values in the data. Only scalar (with or without covariates) bernoulli, poisson and gaussian emission distribution models are supported for now. The `NA` values are ignored in the likelihood calculations, and the corresponding entries in the data are not used to update the parameters of the model. Multivariate models, and missing values in the edge covariates, now stop with an explicit error.
* Gaussian models with `NA`: the variance and the likelihood now use the number of observed entries, and the covariates of `BM_gaussian_covariates` on the missing entries are ignored. The residuals used for the initializations are null on the missing entries.
* `BM_gaussian` with SBM and SBM_sym memberships: the variance is now the mean squared error over the n(n-1) entries out of the diagonal (it was divided by n^2), consistently with the likelihood. Its estimate is multiplied by n/(n-1) compared to the previous versions.
* Fixed `prediction()` of `BM_bernoulli_covariates` and `BM_bernoulli_covariates_fast`: the expected network is now the expectation under the posterior probabilities, sum over (q,l) of Z[i,q] Z[j,l] sigmo(m[q,l] + covariates effect), instead of sigmo(Z m Z' + covariates effect), which was exact only for indicator memberships (#2). As the residuals guide the initializations by splitting groups, the estimates of these models may slightly differ from the previous versions.
* Added a `NEWS.md` file to track changes to the package.
